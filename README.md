# OptimaNova AI Tech Corps — Nonprofit AI Automation Capstone

## Project Overview
A team capstone focused on improving nonprofit workflows through AI-assisted automation, responsible implementation, and staff enablement.

**Program:** AI Tech Corps — OptimaNova AI  
**My Role:** Enable Phase Lead — Staff Training, Documentation & Responsible AI

## The Problem
Nonprofit staff spend valuable time on manual administrative work, including managing records, preparing communications, and reviewing information. These workflows can create delays, repetitive tasks, and risks when sensitive information is handled without clear safeguards.

## Our Solution
Our team developed an AI-assisted workflow concept using Make.com, structured data, and Gemini to support nonprofit operations.

The solution emphasizes:
- Workflow automation
- AI-assisted content generation
- Human review before external communications
- Error handling and validation
- Responsible AI governance

## My Individual Contributions
I led the Enable phase, focusing on making the solution practical, safe, and understandable for staff.

My responsibilities included:
- Developing staff training and handoff documentation
- Defining human-review safeguards
- Documenting troubleshooting and escalation procedures
- Providing responsible AI and data-handling guidance
- Supporting workflow testing and operational readiness

## Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards
AI-generated communications should be reviewed and approved by an authorized staff member before being sent.

The proposed safeguards also address missing records, duplicate entries, unexpected workflow errors, and sensitive information.

## Before and After Results
| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Communication preparation | Manual workflow | AI-assisted draft preparation |
| Approval process | Manual review | Human approval checkpoint |
| Error handling | Manual investigation | Documented validation and troubleshooting procedures |
| Staff handoff | Informal guidance | Structured training and handoff documentation |
